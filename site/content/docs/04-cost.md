## Per turn

The meter above the chat shows what the current turn and the panel have cost so far.

## Budgets

**Budgets** (from `Cmd K`) sets limits for one panel, and per day for a project folder and a persona.
A task has its own budget.
Before a turn starts, the app estimates it from the recent turns and checks the limits; a turn that
would cross one is held and the panel says why.

## Spend and Savings

**Spend** shows what your subscriptions and APIs cost this month, in one total. **Savings** shows the
tokens the app kept out of Claude's context, by source.

Exchange rates in Spend are fixed until you press **Update rates**.

You can also set an amount above which a single turn asks before it runs, and the share of a limit at
which the panel warns you.
